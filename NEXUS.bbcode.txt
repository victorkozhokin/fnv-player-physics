[size=5][b]Description:[/b][/size]
Forked and redoing of [url=https://github.com/AltimorTASDK/fnv-player-physics]AltimorTASDK's fnv-player-physics[/url]

Replaces the player's movement with Quake-style physics:
real ground friction,
air control,
air strafing
and bunny hops.

The character stops sliding around like a shopping trolley and starts going where you point it.

[size=5][b]Features:[/b][/size]
[list]
You accelerate!
You slow down!
You can air strafe!
You can bunny hop!
Landings hurt your speed, not just your health!
3 presets — Responsive, Grounded, Heavy!
Full MCM menu, everything applies without a restart!
No ESP (ESPLESS)!
[/list]

Works great with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/85459]Enhanced Movement[/url]

[size=5][b]Compatabilities:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/93642]MCM Extender[/url] (optional — for the config menu)
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/71373]Walking Inertia[/url] does the same job for the same reason — you do not need both, but they will not fight!
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Install with MO2
[/list]

Enjoy! ^_^
