[size=5][b]Description:[/b][/size]
Replaces the player's movement with Quake-style physics: real ground friction, air control, air strafing and bunny hops. The character stops sliding around like a shopping trolley and starts going where you point it.

[size=5][b]Features:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]You accelerate!
[*]You slow down!
[*]You can air strafe!
[*]You can bunny hop!
[*]Landings hurt your speed, not just your health!
[*]3 presets — Responsive, Grounded, Heavy!
[*]Full MCM menu, everything applies without a restart!
[*]No ESP!
[/list]

Works great with [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/85459]Enhanced Movement[/url]
Walkin' Inertia does the same job for the same reason — you do not need both, but they will not fight!

[size=5][b]Requirements:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]xNVSE
[*]FalloutNV.exe 1.4.0.525 (Steam or GOG)
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/93642]MCM Extender[/url] (optional — for the menu)
[/list]

[size=5][b]Installation:[/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Install with MO2
[*]MCM-RU folder is optional Russian menu text
[/list]

[b]Note:[/b] the plugin refuses to load on any other exe build, on purpose — every address it patches is hardcoded, and loading anyway would just crash you later.

Enjoy! (◡‿◡)
