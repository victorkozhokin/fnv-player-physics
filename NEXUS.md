Description:
Replaces the player's movement with Quake-style physics: real ground friction,
air control, air strafing and bunny hops. The character stops sliding around
like a shopping trolley and starts going where you point it.

Features:

You accelerate!
You slow down!
You can air strafe!
You can bunny hop!
Landings hurt your speed, not just your health!
3 presets — Responsive, Grounded, Heavy!
Full MCM menu, everything applies without a restart!
No ESP!

Works great with Enhanced Movement﻿
Walkin' Inertia does the same job for the same reason — you do not need both,
but they will not fight!

Requirements:

xNVSE
FalloutNV.exe 1.4.0.525 (Steam or GOG)
MCM Extender (optional — for the menu)

Installation:

Install with MO2
MCM-RU folder is optional Russian menu text

Note: the plugin refuses to load on any other exe build, on purpose — every
address it patches is hardcoded, and loading anyway would just crash you later.

Enjoy! (◡‿◡)
